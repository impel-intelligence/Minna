title: Halloway Grant Reporting Timeline
summary: Reporting obligations and deadlines under the Halloway grant, including the milestone that governs the second tranche of funding.
---

The Halloway grant runs for thirty months and disburses in three tranches. The first tranche was released on signature. The remaining two are milestone-gated rather than time-gated.

%%

Release of the second tranche requires the interim technical report plus a demonstration that the pilot skid has run continuously for 500 hours. The 500 hour requirement is the binding constraint, not the report.

%%

The interim technical report is due at month fourteen. Financial reconciliation is due separately at month twelve, and the two deadlines are frequently confused because the reporting portal lists them under a single heading.

%%

Underspend of up to fifteen percent may be carried across tranche boundaries without prior approval. Anything above fifteen percent requires written agreement from the programme office before the tranche closes.
