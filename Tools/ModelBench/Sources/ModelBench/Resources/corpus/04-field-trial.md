title: Kestrel Valley Winter Wheat Field Trial
summary: Replicated field trial of winter wheat varieties across twenty plots, reporting grain yield and lodging incidence.
---

The trial comprised twenty plots arranged in four randomised blocks at the Kestrel Valley station. Each plot measured 12 by 30 metres. Sowing took place in the second week of October at a seed rate of 180 kilograms per hectare.

%%

Plot 14, sown with the Harrowdown variety, returned the highest grain yield of the trial at 6.2 tonnes per hectare. The block mean was 5.1 tonnes per hectare, so Harrowdown outperformed the site average by more than a tonne.

%%

Lodging was recorded in six plots following the storm in the third week of June. All six were sown with the taller Merrivale variety. No Harrowdown plot lodged, despite Merrivale and Harrowdown being sown at the same rate.

%%

Soil nitrogen at sowing averaged 42 kilograms per hectare across the site, with the eastern block measuring notably lower at 31 kilograms per hectare. The eastern block also produced the lowest yields, which we attribute primarily to this nitrogen deficit rather than to variety.
