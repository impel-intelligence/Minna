title: Precious Metals Supplier Audit, Second Half
summary: Audit of precious metal suppliers covering traceability, assay variance, and batch assignments for the reporting period.
---

This audit covers the three suppliers qualified to provide palladium and platinum group metals for pilot plant use during the reporting period. Each was assessed on assay variance, documentation completeness, and delivery reliability.

%%

Thal Metallurgical supplied the palladium used in batches VR-2201 through VR-2240. Their assay variance was the tightest of the three at plus or minus 0.03 percent, and all certificates of analysis were complete on first submission.

%%

Okonkwo Refining supplied the platinum feedstock and a small quantity of rhodium. Their documentation was complete but two deliveries arrived outside the agreed temperature envelope, which we have raised as a corrective action.

%%

Brightwater Metals remains on probationary status. Assay variance reached plus or minus 0.4 percent on one lot, and we have suspended their allocation until a repeat audit is completed. No pilot plant batch used Brightwater material during this period.
