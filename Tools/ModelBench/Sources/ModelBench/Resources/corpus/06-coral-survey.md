title: Marrow Reef Bleaching Survey
summary: Transect survey of coral bleaching severity at Marrow Reef, including the temperature threshold at which bleaching onset was observed.
---

Twelve permanent transects were surveyed at Marrow Reef, each 50 metres long and assessed by two independent observers. Bleaching severity was scored on the standard six point scale at every metre mark.

%%

Bleaching onset was observed once sea surface temperature exceeded 29.4 degrees Celsius for more than eight consecutive days. Below that threshold no transect showed severity above score two, regardless of how long the warm period lasted.

%%

Acropora colonies bleached first and most severely, reaching mean severity score five on the northern transects. Porites colonies on the same transects held at score two, confirming the genus level difference reported in earlier surveys.

%%

Recovery was assessed twelve weeks after the warm period ended. Northern transects recovered to mean severity score two, while the southern transects, which had experienced a shorter warm period, returned to score one.
