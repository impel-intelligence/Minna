title: Shift Worker Sleep Cohort, Interim Analysis
summary: Interim analysis of a sleep cohort of rotating shift workers, covering recruitment, sleep efficiency, and dropout.
---

The cohort recruited 312 rotating shift workers from three hospital sites. Participants wore wrist actigraphy for eight weeks and completed a sleep diary each morning. Recruitment ran for five months.

%%

Mean sleep efficiency across the cohort was 78 percent. Participants on the night rotation averaged 71 percent, while those on the early rotation averaged 84 percent. The gap between rotations was stable across all three sites.

%%

Dropout reached 19 percent by week six, concentrated almost entirely among night rotation participants. Exit interviews cited device discomfort rather than diary burden as the main reason for withdrawal.

%%

We have not yet unblinded the intervention arm, so no statement about the effect of the sleep hygiene programme is possible from this interim analysis. The final analysis is scheduled after the eight week follow up completes.
