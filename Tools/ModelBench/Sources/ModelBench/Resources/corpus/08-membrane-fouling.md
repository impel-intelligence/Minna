title: Membrane Fouling in the Pilot Filtration Skid
summary: Fouling behaviour of ultrafiltration membranes on the pilot skid, including the cleaning interval and the flux at which fouling accelerated.
---

The pilot skid runs four ultrafiltration modules in parallel, each with a nominal 0.02 micron pore size. Feed is clarified process water from the upstream settling basin.

%%

Transmembrane pressure rose slowly up to a flux of 65 litres per square metre per hour. Above that flux the pressure rise steepened sharply, roughly tripling in rate, which we take as the onset of irreversible fouling.

%%

The cleaning interval was set at 72 hours of operation. Extending it to 96 hours in a trial run left residual transmembrane pressure 18 percent above baseline after cleaning, so we reverted to 72 hours.

%%

Caustic cleaning recovered flux more effectively than acid cleaning for this feed, restoring 96 percent of baseline flux against 81 percent for acid. A combined caustic then acid sequence gave no further improvement over caustic alone.
